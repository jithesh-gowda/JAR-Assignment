# JAR App Assignment – Data Analysis & Business Expansion Proposal

This repository contains my complete assignment for the **JAR App Business Analyst / Intern role**.  
It includes datasets, analysis code, insights, visualizations, and a detailed PDF report.

## 📘 Contents of the Assignment

### **1. Data Analysis (Python – Jupyter Notebook)**
The notebook includes:
- Merging datasets for sales & profitability analysis  
- Category-wise sales, profit margin, and performance insights  
- Month-over-month target analysis  
- State-level sales, profit, and disparity insights  
- Visualizations (matplotlib) for better understanding

---

### **2. Business Insights & Recommendations**
- Evaluation of high/low performing categories  
- Regional disparities in profit & order count  
- Pricing improvement suggestions  
- Target alignment insights  
- Jar App feature review (what works & areas of improvement)

---

### **3. New Business Opportunities for Jar App**
The report highlights future expansion ideas:
1. Diversifying into other digital assets (Silver, Mutual Funds, ETFs)  
2. Loan against invested gold  
3. Round-off integration with UPI/payment apps  
4. Short-term goal-based savings  

These opportunities leverage Jar’s strengths in automation, trust, and ease of use.
